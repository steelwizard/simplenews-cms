Sync adds every category and page that has no entry in its chosen menu (pages on the top level, hidden pages stay out of the public menu until published), re-nests entries marked "auto" when their category moved, and removes entries whose page, category or review type was deleted. Editing an "auto" entry turns it into a manual one that sync leaves alone. To keep a category or page out of the menu, hide its entry instead of deleting it, or sync will add it again. The sidebar already shows the full category tree by default.
